# Promising Real-World Bitcoin Payment Examples

Compiled from the X thread started by [@robin_linus](https://x.com/robin_linus/status/2046777611997634799) (April 2026):  
*"What are the most promising examples of Bitcoin payments actually working in the real world?"*

---

## Circular Economies & Bitcoin Communities

These are locations or projects where Bitcoin (often via Lightning) is used as everyday money among locals and visitors.

- **Bitcoin Beach** (El Zonte & surrounding areas, El Salvador)  
  One of the original circular economies. Locals use Lightning for food, services, utilities, and more.  
  → [bitcoinbeach.com](https://www.bitcoinbeach.com/)

- **Bitcoin Jungle** (Uvita / Dominical / Ojochal “Golden Triangle”, Costa Rica)  
  Organic community project with 600+ merchants accepting Lightning payments (zero fees for merchants).  
  → [bitcoinjungle.app](https://www.bitcoinjungle.app/)

- **Lugano Plan B** (Switzerland)  
  City-level initiative making Lugano a Bitcoin hub; hundreds of merchants accept Bitcoin/Lightning + local digital currency.

- **South Africa communities**  
  - Bitcoin Witsand  
  - Bitcoin Ekasi (Mossel Bay)  
  - Plettenberg Bay and others  
  Supported by MoneyBadger bridging into local QR systems.

- **Other circular economies mentioned**  
  - Witsand / Ekasi / Victoria Falls  
  - Motiv projects in Peru  
  - BTC Cuba  
  - Agbozume (Ghana)  
  - Curaçao  
  - Free Madeira (Portugal)  
  - Rolante and many others in Brazil  
  - Vancouver, BC (100+ pins on BTC Map)  
  - Paraguay Bitcoin community  
  - Bitcoin communities in Benin, Mexico, Philippines, etc.

## Physical Retail & Merchant Acceptance

Chains and individual merchants that accept Bitcoin (on-chain or Lightning) directly or via processors.

- **Square terminals / Square payments** (USA)  
  Widespread point-of-sale support for Bitcoin payments.

- **Steak ’n Shake**  
  Accepts Bitcoin.

- **Spar supermarkets** (Switzerland)  
  Accept Bitcoin.

- **Sheetz** (USA convenience stores)  
  Accepts Bitcoin; reported 50% discount when paying with BTC.

- **AMC Theatres & Regal Cinemas** (USA)  
  Accept Bitcoin.

- **Chipotle & Home Depot** (USA)  
  Accept via the same payment processors used by Sheetz (noted as less seamless).

- **AT&T** (USA)  
  Accepts Bitcoin.  
  → [AT&T support article](https://www.att.com/support/article/my-account/KM1009340/)

- **Namecheap**  
  Domain registrar with long-standing BTCPay Server integration; processed >$73M in BTC revenue.  
  → [BTCPay Server case study](https://blog.btcpayserver.org/case-study-namecheap/)

- **Le Bon Vieux Temps** (Montreal bar)  
  Accepts Lightning via EclairBTC / BTCPay Server + Liquid + Boltz (no-KYC, direct-to-wallet).

- Various independent merchants and bars/restaurants powered by **BTCPay Server** or local POS solutions (e.g. Nerdminerstore in Europe).

## Payment Apps & Local Spending Solutions (Especially Emerging Markets)

Tools that let people spend Bitcoin at merchants who don’t natively accept it (via QR bridging, USSD, mobile money, etc.).

- **Machankura**  
  USSD-based Bitcoin (Lightning) payments that work on feature phones with no internet or smartphone required. Available in:  
  Côte d’Ivoire, Ghana, Kenya, Malawi, Namibia, Nigeria, South Africa, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia.  
  Ideal for circular economies and unbanked users.

- **Pleb QR** (Thailand)  
  Pay *any* Thai PromptPay QR code with Lightning Bitcoin. Peer-to-peer matching: you pay Lightning, a local pays the merchant in THB and receives the sats.  
  → [plebqr.com](https://plebqr.com/)

- **Tando** (Kenya)  
  Spend Bitcoin anywhere M-Pesa is accepted. Send BTC → recipient gets KES (or vice versa). Zero fees for users in many cases.  
  → [tando.me](https://tando.me/)

- **MoneyBadger** (South Africa)  
  Infrastructure that lets merchants accept Bitcoin/Lightning (settled in Rand). Integrated with major wallets and deployed to 1,600+ outlets including Pick n Pay.  
  → [moneybadger.co.za](https://www.moneybadger.co.za/)

- **Other African payment apps / services**  
  - MavaPay (Nigeria)  
  - BitSpenda (Ghana)  
  - Banxaas (Senegal)  
  - BitZed (Zambia)  
  - Tapnob (Nigeria)  
  - FediBTC, Bitnob, Minmo (multi-country Africa)  
  - Chapsmart (Tanzania – Bitcoin → M-Pesa)  
  - BTC Flash (Benin)  
  - UlendoApp

- **Philippines**  
  Local apps such as BTC Isle used for rent, food, phone bills, etc.

- **Strike bill-pay**  
  Pay mortgages, credit cards, college tuition, and many other bills with Bitcoin.

## Online Services, Gift Cards & Digital Goods

High-volume, frictionless spending that works globally.

- **Bitrefill**  
  Gift cards, phone refills, eSIMs, and bill payments worldwide. Major Lightning usage.  
  → [bitrefill.com](https://www.bitrefill.com/)

- **Azteco**  
  Bitcoin vouchers (like prepaid cards) sold online and in physical stores. Easy on-ramp for small amounts.  
  → [azte.co](https://azte.co/)

- **Travala** – Travel bookings with Bitcoin  
- **ShopinBit**  
- **Mullvad VPN** – Accepts Bitcoin  
- **Proton** – Accepts Bitcoin (on-chain only at time of thread)  
- **silent.link** – Privacy-focused eSIMs  
  → [silent.link](https://silent.link)
- **Nadanada** [nadanada.me](https://nadanada.me/)

- Other frequently cited high-volume categories: online gambling, supplements, phone cards, seedboxes, Nostr zaps.

## Tools, Infrastructure & Maps

- **BTC Map**  
  Open, community-maintained map of merchants accepting Bitcoin worldwide (based on OpenStreetMap). Apps available for web, Android, and iOS.  
  → [btcmap.org](https://btcmap.org/)

- **BTCPay Server**  
  Self-hosted, open-source payment processor used by many of the merchants and projects above (Namecheap, independent shops, circular economies).

- **EclairBTC** and similar no-KYC Lightning POS solutions.

- **HRF & OpenSats grants**  
  Funding real-world Bitcoin usage, education, and circular economy projects.

---

## Notes

- Many of the strongest examples combine **Lightning Network** with local payment rails (M-Pesa, PromptPay, South African QR systems, USSD).
- Circular economies and emerging-market apps are repeatedly cited as the most “actually working” cases of everyday Bitcoin use.
- Online services (Bitrefill, Azteco, VPNs, etc.) often process higher volumes than pure in-person retail in developed countries.
- The list is not exhaustive — the thread noted that new circular economies and merchant integrations appear regularly.
